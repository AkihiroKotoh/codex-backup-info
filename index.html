Codex Backup

A personal backup configuration using rclone to copy selected files from a local computer to Google Drive. This is a personal-use setup, not a service accepting registrations or files from other users.

Why this app exists

Rclone’s shared Google Drive app credentials are being retired during 2026. Its official guidance directs users to create personal Google app credentials to avoid interruption. This app supplies that identity for an existing rclone backup configuration.

The backup method and existing uploaded files remain the same. Changing the app credentials does not itself require uploading the backups again.

Google’s Testing mode limits authorizations to one week. These information pages support moving the personal app out of Testing for regular backups. Publishing these pages does not publish the backup files.

Official rclone setup guidance

Google authorization allows the local rclone application to access an authorized Drive account. Access can be reviewed and revoked through Google Account settings.
